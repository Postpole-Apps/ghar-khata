# Title

Ghar Khata - Milk & Maid Bill

## Short description

Mark milk, maid & newspaper days and get your monthly bill in one tap.

## Full description

Ghar Khata is a simple home diary for everyone who comes to your door: the milkman, your housekeeper, the newspaper delivery and the water delivery. Mark the days, and the monthly bill adds itself up.

MILK DIARY WITHOUT THE NOTEBOOK
Everything is "present" by default. Tap only the days something changed: no milk today, half a liter extra, a double delivery. Your milk diary keeps the liters and the rate, and the monthly bill is ready on the 1st.

MAID ATTENDANCE AND SALARY
Set a monthly salary and paid leaves. Ghar Khata counts absences and works out the salary for the month. Change the salary later and old months keep their old amount.

NEWSPAPER, WATER DELIVERY AND MORE
Newspaper copies per day, water deliveries, cook or driver visits: pick per unit, per visit or monthly.

HOME-SCREEN WIDGET (FREE)
- A home-screen widget shows today's list and lets you mark what changed.

SHARE THE MONTHLY BILL
Send the bill as text on WhatsApp. Pro adds a bill image or PDF.

PRIVATE AND SIMPLE
Your data is stored on your device. No account, no sign-up. The free version shows ads; you can remove them with a one-time purchase. Ad and data practices are described in the Privacy Policy; in regions that require it (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland), ad choices are also available under Settings > Privacy options. Back up your data to a file you choose, and restore it any time. Backup is always free. Usage analytics and crash reports (not your diary entries) are described in the Privacy Policy and Data safety section.

FREE
Marking days, rates, paid leaves, the monthly bill and backup are free for up to 3 providers.

PRO (ONE-TIME PURCHASE, NO SUBSCRIPTION)
Unlimited providers, bill as image or PDF, CSV export, year summary, a daily "mark today" reminder. Pro also removes ads.

REMOVE ADS (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
Just want the ads gone? Remove ads hides them. Pro features stay locked.

Available in English and Hindi (follows your phone's language).
